Cast AI’s PrecisionPack rightsizes workloads based on actual container usage, reducing overprovisioned CPU and memory requests while increasing underprovisioned ones. By aligning resource requests with real demand, it helps autoscalers provision fewer resources and can significantly reduce OOM kills.
