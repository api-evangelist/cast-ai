GPU cost monitoring tracks how much of each expensive GPU is actually used and attributes that cost to the teams, workloads, and applications consuming it. Since GPUs average about 5% utilization, visibility is the first step: you cannot reclaim idle GPU spend you cannot see or assign to an owner.
